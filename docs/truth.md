Each case is one chaser and one target. The stages are the ones the detector raises: plane (the chaser within a fraction of a degree of the target's plane, by launch or by manoeuvre, with the separation where a source gives it), height (the semi-major axes matched), beside (the chaser holding near the target), closest (the nearest pass stated, in kilometres) and left (the chaser moving away). Each cell is the window the finest source states, with its precision where it is not a day; a blank is a stage no source states. The owner is GCAT's owner code and state. The confidence is A where a tracking company, an operator or a government states the day of the hold or of a measured pass, B where any source states a day, C a month, D a year.
